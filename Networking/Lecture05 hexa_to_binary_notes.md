# Hexadecimal to Binary Conversion — Notes

## 4 Binary Bits ke Weights
```
8 | 4 | 2 | 1
```
Ye 4 boxes hi binary ki position values hain. Har box ke liye poochho:
**"Kya mujhe ye value chahiye?" → Haan = 1, Nahi = 0**

### Example: 6
6 = 4 + 2
| 8 | 4 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 | 1 | 0 |

**6 = 0110**

Check: 0×8 + 1×4 + 1×2 + 0×1 = 6 ✅

## IPv6 Address Example

**Original Address:**
```
2001:0DB8:85A3:0000:0000:8A2E:0370:7334
```

**Rule:** Har group mein 4 hex digits hote hain, aur har hex digit = 4 binary bits.
Isliye har group = 16 bits.

### Group-by-Group Conversion

**2001**
| Digit | 2 | 0 | 0 | 1 |
|-------|---|---|---|---|
| Binary | 0010 | 0000 | 0000 | 0001 |

→ `0010 0000 0000 0001`

**0DB8**
| Digit | 0 | D | B | 8 |
|-------|---|---|---|---|
| Binary | 0000 | 1101 | 1011 | 1000 |

→ `0000 1101 1011 1000`

**85A3**
| Digit | 8 | 5 | A | 3 |
|-------|---|---|---|---|
| Binary | 1000 | 0101 | 1010 | 0011 |

→ `1000 0101 1010 0011`

**0000**
→ `0000 0000 0000 0000`

**0000**
→ `0000 0000 0000 0000`

**8A2E**
| Digit | 8 | A | 2 | E |
|-------|---|---|---|---|
| Binary | 1000 | 1010 | 0010 | 1110 |

→ `1000 1010 0010 1110`

**0370**
| Digit | 0 | 3 | 7 | 0 |
|-------|---|---|---|---|
| Binary | 0000 | 0011 | 0111 | 0000 |

→ `0000 0011 0111 0000`

**7334**
| Digit | 7 | 3 | 3 | 4 |
|-------|---|---|---|---|
| Binary | 0111 | 0011 | 0011 | 0100 |

→ `0111 0011 0011 0100`
